# Organization identities

Used for identification in the satellite launch provenance card. Logos remain
the marks of their respective organizations; no affiliation is implied.

- `spacex.ico`: SpaceX's identity icon, downloaded 2026-10-25 from
  https://www.spacex.com/assets/favicon-spacex.ico (linked by the official home page).
- `nasa.svg`: official NASA insignia, downloaded 2026-10-25 from
  https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/themes/nasa/assets/images/nasa-logo.svg.
  Also identifies NASA centers JSC, MSFC and GSFC by their explicit GCAT codes.

Organizations without a verified local asset display their catalog name only.
Assets are served locally, with no third-party logo requests from the browser.
