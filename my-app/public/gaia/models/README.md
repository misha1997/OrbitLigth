# Gaia schematic

`gaia.glb` is an original educational model created by OrbitLight with
`node scripts/build-gaia-model.cjs`. It depicts the deployed sunshield,
spacecraft body and two telescope apertures in simplified form.

The silhouette and 10 m sunshield diameter are based on the
[ESA Gaia factsheet](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/Gaia/Gaia_factsheet).
Other shapes and proportions are illustrative. This is not an ESA engineering
model and must not be presented as one.
